Improve search ranking, add curated collections and in-app preview

Search
- Keep the top 24 results in the engine's own relevance order, and apply
  the folder interleave and orientation/people grouping only below them.
  Both were reordering the whole list, so a qualifier like "nighttime"
  survived in only the first few results before daytime shots took over.
- Choose the ordering from the query: one asking for a thing ("burger")
  leads with close-ups, one asking for a scene ("birthday party") leads
  with guests. Decided by comparing the query against two concept
  directions rather than a word list, which would not survive a menu in
  two languages.
- Add reference-image search: the query is embedded from a picture
  instead of words and ranked identically.

Collections
- Collections are curated by hand rather than classified automatically:
  drag a photo onto one, or right-click it. Empty collections stay in the
  sidebar because they are the drop targets.

Favourites
- Stop the sync merging this machine's own shared file back in. An
  un-starred favourite returned through the team list and could never be
  removed.

Interface

  freezing while several hundred widgets are built.

  match the resolution actually cached.
- Preview in-app on double-click, sharpening to an 800px copy as it
  arrives; Copy puts the full-resolution original on the clipboard.

  a sync that disabled searching, which is the opposite of what it said.
- Make the Enter key honour the busy state it was bypassing.
- Stop CustomTkinter rescaling every widget when the window moves to a
